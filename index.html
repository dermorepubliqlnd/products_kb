Skincare Goals

Every Dermorepubliq product is formulated around one of eight core skincare goals. Use these to guide customers to the right products.

Renew

Resurface and revitalize by accelerating cell turnover. Addresses dull skin, uneven texture, and congested pores through exfoliation.

Key actives: Salicylic Acid, Glycolic Acid, Mandelic Acid, Papaya Extract

Brighten

Fade dark spots, hyperpigmentation, and uneven tone. Inhibit melanin production for a visibly luminous, even complexion.

Key actives: Niacinamide, Alpha Arbutin, Tranexamic Acid, Glutathione, Vitamin C, Kojic Acid

Calm

Soothe redness, reduce inflammation, and comfort reactive or sensitive skin. Strengthen the barrier to prevent future irritation.

Key actives: Calendula, Chamomile, Centella Asiatica, low-dose Niacinamide

Hydrate

Replenish and lock in moisture at multiple skin layers. Address tightness, flakiness, and dehydration lines for plump, comfortable skin.

Key actives: Hyaluronic Acid, Snail Mucin, Jeju Aloe, Glycerin, Sodium Hyaluronate

Repair

Rebuild and strengthen a compromised skin barrier. Ideal for skin that is dry, sensitized, or recovering from over-exfoliation or treatment.

Key actives: Ceramide, Vitamin B5 (Panthenol), Oat Extract, Centella Asiatica

Clarify

Control excess sebum, minimize pores, and prevent breakouts. Maintain clear skin through anti-acne and oil-regulating actives.

Key actives: Niacinamide, Salicylic Acid, Potassium Azeloyl Diglycinate, Witch Hazel

Rewind

Reduce visible signs of aging including fine lines, wrinkles, and loss of firmness. Stimulate collagen production and accelerate cell renewal.

Key actives: Retinyl Palmitate, Backuchiol, Peptides, stable Vitamin C

Protect

Shield skin from UVA/UVB radiation and environmental oxidative stress. Prevention is the most effective anti-aging and anti-pigmentation strategy.

Key actives: SPF30 PA++ broad-spectrum filters, mattifying silica agents

How to Use Goals in a Consultation

  1. Ask the customer their #1 skin complaint — map it to a primary Goal.
  2. Ask about secondary concerns — this often leads to a second Goal product.
  3. Check skin type — sensitive skin customers may need the gentler formula variants.
  4. Build a routine — Cleanse → Tone → Treat → Moisturize → Protect (AM).
  5. Start simple — new-to-skincare customers: max 3 products. Add one at a time.

📦 Packaging

Most hydrating toners have spray caps for easy application — just spray directly on the skin after cleansing.

AHA and BHA toners (GLY, SAT, MAT) have screw caps so you can pour the product onto your palm or a cotton pad. This gives better control over application and prevents accidental contact with the eyes, which is important when working with chemical exfoliants.

Thinner serums use dropper caps, while thicker serums use pump caps. This ensures the right amount is dispensed each time and gives customers full control over dosage — important for potent actives like Retinol and Vitamin C.

The amber glass reduces light exposure, which prevents oxidation and extends the product's shelf life. Active ingredients like Vitamin C, AHAs, and Retinol are especially sensitive to light and can lose potency when exposed. This is a quality protection feature, not just aesthetics.

Creams use an airless pump bottle. This mechanism pushes product up from the bottom of the container, ensuring you can use every last drop. It also minimizes air exposure, keeping the formula stable and hygienic throughout its use.

⚙ Understanding Actives

All three are chemical exfoliants that remove dead skin cells, excess oil, and impurities — but they work differently:

Glycolic Acid (GLY) — AHA: Water-soluble. Works on the skin surface. Exfoliates, brightens, and improves texture. Best for normal to dry skin. More potent, start slowly.

Mandelic Acid (MAT) — AHA: Also water-soluble but has a larger molecule size, so it penetrates more gently. Better for sensitive skin or beginners who want AHA benefits with less irritation.

Salicylic Acid (SAT) — BHA: Oil-soluble. Goes deeper into pores, dissolving sebum and dead skin inside them. Best for oily and acne-prone skin. The go-to for blackheads and congestion.

Purging vs. Irritation: Purging appears where you normally break out and clears within 2–4 weeks. Irritation appears in new areas, causes redness/stinging, and does not improve.

Skincare requires patience and consistency. Here are realistic timelines:

Oil control / acne reduction: 2–4 weeks.

Dark spots / brightening: 6–12 weeks minimum with consistent daily use.

Anti-aging / firmness: 12+ weeks.

Allow at least 6–8 weeks of consistent use before re-evaluating. Skipping days resets progress. Consistency is the single biggest factor in results.
